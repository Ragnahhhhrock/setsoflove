# Blog writing brief

Read this, `STYLE_GUIDE.md` and `content/blog/plan.json` before writing. This file governs every post in `content/blog/posts/`.

## Purpose

The blog helps people who train, and who are curious about meeting someone, find honest, useful, specific answers. It should also bring them to SetsOfLove through search. Helpful first, promotional a distant second. Google's spam policy targets pages made at scale that add nothing, so each post must earn its place with concrete, original, practical advice.

## File format

One file per post at `content/blog/posts/<slug>.md`. The slug is the file name and must match `plan.json`.

```
---
title: <the exact title from plan.json>
description: <110 to 155 characters, plain, includes the main keyword naturally, no exclamation marks>
---
Opening paragraph that answers the question directly in 2 to 3 sentences. This is what search results quote.

## A heading that is a question or a clear promise

Body text.
```

- No H1 (the title is the H1). Use `##` for sections and `###` only when needed. Sentence case headings.
- 4 to 7 `##` sections. 800 to 1,100 words. Never under 750.
- Allowed markup: paragraphs, `- ` bullets, `1. ` numbered lists, pipe tables (header row, separator row, rows), **bold**, *italic*, `[text](url)`. Nothing else. No HTML, images, emoji or code blocks.
- Internal links: `[text](/blog/<slug>/)`. Use 2 to 4 per post, to slugs that exist in `plan.json`, placed where they genuinely help the reader. Link text describes the target (not "click here").
- External links: only bare root URLs from this list, and only where they help: `https://www.scamwatch.gov.au/`, `https://www.cyber.gov.au/`, `https://www.esafety.gov.au/`, `https://www.1800respect.org.au/`, `https://www.beyondblue.org.au/`, `https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/`, `https://www.parkrun.com.au/`.
- Do not add a call to action, a "related articles" list or a sign-off about SetsOfLove at the end. The page template adds those. You may mention SetsOfLove in the body where it is relevant, in plain words.

## Voice

Friendly, direct, a little dry, like a good gym regular. Capable adults, no lecturing. Follow `STYLE_GUIDE.md`:

- Australian English (colour, organise, favourite, centre).
- Sentence case everywhere. Numerals for numbers except "one" in running prose. Dates as 7 Oct 2026, times as 6:30 am.
- Short sentences, active voice, plain words. Contractions are fine.
- No exclamation marks. No emoji.
- Never use these words, even in passing: hot, babe, hunk, swipe, match (matches, matching, matched, matchmaker), bro, thirst, guys, ladies.
- Write for every gender and every kind of connection. Say "people", "someone", "they". Never assume who someone is looking for.
- No innuendo about bodies or lifting. No pickup tactics, "tricks", "hacks" or lines that work on people. Consent and respect come first in every post.
- One light, dry joke per post at most, and never in safety advice.
- Do not name other dating apps, gyms, brands, supplements or influencers.

## Substance

- Be specific: example phrasings, a checklist, a decision rule, a short worked example. Avoid filler such as "in today's fast-paced world" or "in this article".
- Do not start with the title restated or with a question you then repeat.
- Do not invent statistics, studies, quotes, survey results, testimonials, named people, case studies or "experts say". If you cannot state a fact reliably, leave it out or say it in general terms.
- Health and fitness: general, cautious, non-judgemental. Suggest a qualified coach, doctor or physio where it matters. No calorie or weight targets, no cutting, no supplements, no steroids, no extreme advice. Never shame bodies or food. Do not frame appearance as a goal of training.
- Safety and scams: accurate, calm, practical. Point to Scamwatch, ReportCyber (via cyber.gov.au) or eSafety where relevant.
- Each post has its own angle (in `plan.json`). Do not repeat sentences or structures from other posts in the batch.
- Include the main keyword in the title (already there), the opening paragraph and at least one heading, naturally. Do not stuff.

## SetsOfLove facts (use only these)

- A profile app for single gym-goers aged 18 or over, at any gym, of any gender (man, woman or non-binary). Members choose who they'd like to meet (any mix of men, women and non-binary people) and what they're after (any mix of dates, a relationship, marriage, friendship, just fun).
- Each approved profile lives at its own link, like `setsoflove.com/sam-t`. Link names use lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens, 3 to 30 characters.
- 1 to 4 photos. The main photo shows the member's face clearly. Gym and workout photos are encouraged. No nudity, nothing sexual, no photos of other people without consent, no screenshots, memes or images with text or logos.
- Profile fields: first name, age, gender, who they'd like to meet, what they're after, suburb, city, country, what they do, how they train, about you, a few words on who they'd like to meet. Optional lifts: bench press, squat, deadlift, overhead press, entered in kg or lb and shown in both.
- Tappable quick picks suggest text for most fields. Members can edit anything.
- Every profile is approved by the admin before it goes live. Any edit takes a live profile offline until it is approved again. Rejections and takedowns come with a note on what to change.
- No messages, comments, likes or algorithm, and no matching. Members decide where to share their link. Introductions happen through friends or at the gym.
- Contact details such as phone numbers and social handles are not allowed in public text fields.
- Members can keep contact details private (social accounts, phone, WhatsApp or email, any mix or none). Someone with a live profile can request it, and the member says yes or no. A no is never announced. There are still no messages.
- Every public profile has a "Report this profile" link. Search engines are told not to list profiles.
- Free, no ads. Gym membership is not checked and lifts are self-reported.
- The tagline is "Mirror selfies welcome." Create an account at `/signup`.
- Never claim SetsOfLove will help someone find love, never quote member numbers, and never describe features that are not listed here (no verification badges, no app store app, no premium plans).
