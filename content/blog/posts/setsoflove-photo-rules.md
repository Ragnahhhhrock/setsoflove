SetsOfLove welcomes gym and workout photos, with a few clear limits. Use 1 to 4 photos, make the main one a clear shot of your face, and keep it to you. No nudity, nothing sexual, no other people without their consent, and no screenshots, memes or images with text or logos. Those dating profile photo rules apply to every member.

## What photos are welcome?

The tagline is "Mirror selfies welcome," and it means it. You don't have to hide that you train. Good options include:

- A training shot, mid-session or between sets.
- You in your usual gym kit, whatever you wear to train.
- A post-session mirror photo.
- A shot from an event, a class or a long walk.
- A relaxed photo from outside the gym, so people see more than one side of you.

You can add 1 to 4 photos in total. Your main photo needs to show your face clearly, because it's the first thing people see and the one that tells them who you are. A mix works best: one clear face photo, plus 1 to 3 that show how you train or spend your time.

## What isn't allowed?

These rules are short, and the admin checks them before a profile goes live.

| Not allowed | What to do instead |
|---|---|
| Nudity or anything sexual | Pick a photo in normal gym kit or everyday clothes |
| Photos of other people without their consent | Crop them out or retake it somewhere quieter |
| Someone else as the main person in a photo | Make sure you're the one in the frame |
| Screenshots | Use the original photo from your camera |
| Memes | Save them for the group chat |
| Images with text or logos overlaid | Use a clean image with nothing added |

Notice how plain these are. None of them say anything about how you look, how big or small you are, or what you should wear. They're about consent, clarity and keeping the space comfortable for everyone.

## Why does the main photo need to show your face?

Because a profile is meant to describe a real person, and a face is the quickest way for someone to recognise you when you meet. If the only photo is a back view, a distant shape, or a face hidden by a cap or sunglasses, it's hard for anyone to know who they're looking at.

The member should be the person in the main photo. That's about trust, and it protects you too. You'll find more on this in [why your main photo should clearly show your face](/blog/main-photo-show-your-face/).

This doesn't mean every photo has to be a close-up. It means the first one is. Once your face is clear, the rest can show you lifting, climbing, walking or doing whatever you're into.

## What about people in the background?

This is where many gym photos run into trouble. Gyms are shared spaces, and the people behind you didn't agree to be in your profile.

Practical fixes:

1. Go at a quiet time, such as early in the morning or outside peak hours.
2. Pick a corner, a stairwell or an empty stretch of mirror.
3. Check the whole frame before you press the button, including the mirror's edges.
4. If someone drifts into shot, retake it. Don't rely on cropping afterwards.
5. If you're not sure, leave it out.

It also helps to think about what's polite in a gym generally. Don't point your camera at other people, even by accident. The full reasoning is in [consent and gym photos: why bystanders stay out](/blog/consent-and-gym-photos/), and tips on getting a clean frame are in [how to take a good mirror selfie for your profile](/blog/mirror-selfie-tips-for-your-profile/).

## What counts as "nothing sexual"?

This one is about tone as much as detail. If a photo's main purpose is to be sexual, or if it would make someone uncomfortable to open at their desk, it doesn't belong here. A post-session mirror shot in normal gym kit is fine. A photo posed to be suggestive is not.

If you're unsure, ask: would I be comfortable if a family member opened this link? If the answer is no, pick another photo. There's no need to overthink it. Plenty of good photos are clearly fine.

## What happens if a photo is turned down?

You'll get a note on what to change. Swap the photo, fix the issue, and send the profile for approval again. Remember that any edit to a live profile takes it offline until it's approved again, so make all your changes at once. [How profile approval works on SetsOfLove](/blog/how-profile-approval-works/) explains the process step by step.

## A last check before you send

- Is there at least one clear photo of your face?
- Are you the main person in every photo?
- Is anyone else identifiable in the background?
- Is there any text, logo, meme or screenshot in the image?
- Does anything in it fall under nudity or sexual content?

If every answer is clean, you're ready. Your photos don't need to be perfect. They need to be clear, recent, and you.
