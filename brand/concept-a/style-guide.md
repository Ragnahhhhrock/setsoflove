# Style guide

How profiles, photos and screens are put together. Voice and copy rules are `STYLE_GUIDE.md` in the repo; this guide covers layout, imagery, status and accessibility, and it follows the logo and tokens in the brand book.

## Profile layout

- Photos lead: 1 to 4 photos in 4:5 frames, the main photo first with the member's face clearly visible.
- Under the photos: first name and age as a `heading`, then suburb, city and country in `body-s` `ink-muted`.
- Lift tiles sit directly under the name block: bench press, squat, deadlift and overhead press. Show kg as a `stat` with the lb value beneath in `body-s`, in the member's chosen unit first. Lifts are optional and self-reported, so omit any tile with no value.
- Then what they do and how they train, then "About you" and "What you're looking for" in `body-l`.
- The profile link (`setsoflove.com/sam-t`) and "Report this profile" close the card.
- There are no like, comment or message controls on a profile, and no contact details in public text fields. Introductions happen through mutual friends or at the gym.

## Photos

- Welcome: training shots, gym kit, a post-session mirror pic. Clear, recent and well lit, and the member is easy to recognise.
- Not allowed: nudity, anything sexual or obscene, other people as the main image, screenshots, memes, and images with overlaid text or logos. Only photos the member has the right to share.
- Never add filters, overlays, stickers or captions to a photo in the app.

## Copy

- Follow `STYLE_GUIDE.md`: Australian English, sentence case, verb-first buttons, no emoji, no exclamation marks except one on a success moment.
- A member's profile text is theirs. We approve it, we don't edit it.
- Quick picks are plain, positive and written in the first person, with no humour, innuendo or contact details. Tapping adds a suggestion and tapping again removes it. Members can always change the text.
- Rejection and takedown notes say what to change, in one or two plain sentences.

## Status and moderation

- New and edited profiles show **Pending approval** (`yellow` badge with `on-yellow` text).
- Approved profiles show **Approved** (`status-approved` badge).
- Rejected or taken-down profiles show **Needs changes** (`status-rejected` badge) with the admin's note beneath.
- An edit takes an approved profile offline until it is approved again. Say so before the member saves.

## Using the logo

- Lockup on `paper`, `surface` or `night`; the small mark below 32px; the app icon for home screens and the favicon source.
- Keep the mark away from red and pink grounds. Full rules are in the brand book under Logo.

## Accessibility

- Body text is 16px (`body`) at minimum. `body-s` is for helper text only.
- Every interactive element has a visible `focus` ring and a target of 44px or more.
- Status is always a word plus an icon.
- Every image has alt text: "SetsOfLove" for the lockup, "SetsOfLove logo" for the mark, and "Photo of [first name]" for profile photos.
- Check any new colour pair for 4.5:1 (3:1 for text 24px and up, borders and icons) in both themes before adding it.
