# Style guide

How profiles, photos and copy should look and read. The product rules come from the scope and content policy; the visual rules come from this system.

## Profile layout

- Photos lead. 1 to 4 photos, 4:5 frames, the main photo first with the member's face clearly visible.
- Under the photos: first name and age as a `heading`, then suburb, city and country in `body-s` `ink-muted`.
- Lift stats sit directly under the name block, next to the photos' story: bench press, squat, deadlift and overhead press. Show kg as a `stat` with the lb value beneath in `body-s`. Lifts are optional and self-reported, so omit any tile with no value.
- Then what they do and how they train, then "About you" and "What I'm looking for" in `body-l`.
- The profile link (`setsoflove.com/sam-t`) and "Report this profile" close the card.

## Photos

- Welcome: training shots, gym kit, a post-session mirror pic.
- Not allowed: nudity, anything sexual or obscene, other people who can be recognised without their consent, screenshots, memes, and images with text or logos.
- The member must be the person in the main photo.
- Do not add overlays, stickers or captions to photos. A "photo of a lift" label is parked for a later version.

## Copy

- Friendly and dry. The product can be funny; a member's profile text is theirs and stays unedited by us.
- Keep contact details out of public text fields. Introductions happen through mutual friends or at the gym, so no copy should invite messages, likes or comments.
- Quick-pick suggestions follow the same voice and `STYLE_GUIDE.md`. Tapping adds the text, tapping again removes it, and the member can edit it freely.
- Rejection and takedown notes say what to change, in one or two plain sentences.

## Status and moderation

- New and edited profiles show **Pending approval** (`gold` badge).
- Approved profiles show **Live** (`status-live` badge).
- Rejected or taken-down profiles show **Needs changes** (`status-rejected` badge) with the admin's note.
- An edit takes a live profile offline until it is approved again. Say so before the member saves.

## Accessibility

- Body text is 16px (`body`) at least; `body-s` is for helper text only.
- Every interactive element has a visible `focus` ring and a target of 44px or more.
- Status is always a word plus an icon.
- Check any new colour pair for 4.5:1 (3:1 for text 24px+ and for borders and icons) in both themes before adding it.
