The SetsOfLove logo: the heart emoji with the flex emoji centred in it, on its own or in a horizontal lockup with the wordmark.

**The consumer provides** the ground. Use the logo on `paper`, `surface` or `night`; never on `brand`, `brand-tint` or any red or pink ground, where the heart disappears.

- Light grounds use `setsoflove-lockup.png`; dark grounds use `setsoflove-lockup-reversed.png`. Don't retype the wordmark.
- Wordmark: Bricolage Grotesque 800, -0.02em tracking, "SetsOf" in `ink` (or `paper`), "Love" in `brand`.
- Lockup: mark about 1.35 times the wordmark font size tall, gap about 0.36 times it.
- Clear space: a quarter of the mark's width on every side.
- Below 32px wide use `setsoflove-mark-small.png`.

**Don't** change the skin tone or either glyph, rotate or flip the mark, add a shadow or outline, or place it on a photo.
