The SetsOfLove logo: the mark (coral heart with a cream flexed bicep) on its own, or in a horizontal lockup with the wordmark.

**The consumer provides** the ground. Use the lockup on `paper`, `night` or `surface`; never on a coral ground, where the heart disappears.

- Wordmark: Bricolage Grotesque 800, -0.02em tracking. "SetsOf" in `ink` (or `night`/`paper` on a fixed panel), "Love" in `brand`.
- Lockup: mark about 1.6 times the wordmark font size, gap about 0.35 times it.
- Clear space: a quarter of the mark's width on every side.
- Below 32px and for favicons use `setsoflove-mark-simple.svg`.

**Don't** recolour the heart, rotate the mark, add a drop shadow, or set the wordmark in another typeface.
