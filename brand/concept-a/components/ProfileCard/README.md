The public profile card for one member: photos first, then name, place, lifts, text and the profile link.

**The consumer provides** 1 to 4 photos (the main one showing the member's face clearly), first name, age, suburb, city, country and the profile link stub. Optional: what they do, how they train and the four lifts. Required text: about you and what you're looking for.

- Order: photo, name and age (`heading`), place (`body-s`, `ink-muted`), lift tiles (see LiftStat), what they do and how they train, then the two profile texts in `body-l`.
- Card: `surface-raised`, 1px `line`, `radius-lg`, no shadow. Photo frame is 4:5 with no overlays.
- Ends with the profile link and a ghost "Report this profile" button.
- There are no like, comment or message controls on a profile, and no contact details in text fields.

**Don't** crop out the face in the main photo, place text over a photo, or show any control that invites unsolicited contact.
