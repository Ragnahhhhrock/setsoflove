Photo guidelines
  • Gym and workout photos are welcome. No nudity, and nothing sexual or obscene.
  • No sunglasses or hats shading your face in your main photo.
  • Only photos you have the right to share. No photos of other people as the main image.
  • No screenshots, memes, or images with text or logos on them.
