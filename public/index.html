What goes on your profile

  • 1 to 4 photos, with a clear shot of your face as the main one
  • First name, age, suburb and city
  • What you do and how you train
  • About you and what you're looking for
  • Your own link, like setsoflove.com/sam-t

Short on words? Tap a quick pick under each field and edit it to suit.

How it works

Create your profile

Add a few photos and a few lines about you. It takes about 10 minutes.

We approve it

We check every profile before it goes live. If you change it later, we check it again.

Share your link

You get your own link. Send it to whoever you like.

Keep it friendly and keep it clean

  • Gym and workout photos are welcome. Nothing sexual, nothing obscene
  • You must be the person in your main photo
  • No photos of other people without their consent
  • No phone numbers or social handles in your profile
  • No comments, likes or messages, so nobody gets unwanted remarks on their photos

Introductions happen through mutual friends or at the gym. Every profile has a link to report it.
